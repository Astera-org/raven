# Comments can only be edited briefly

- **Decision**: you can edit your comment for one hour after posting, as long as nobody has replied to it.
- **Why**: comments are part of the scientific record. A reply should always respond to the words that are on the page. The one-hour window still lets you fix a typo or a mistake you notice right after posting.
