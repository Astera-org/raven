# Product decisions

The Stacks is an experiment in scientific communication, and product decisions need to balance the following principles:

- Authors should have control of what and when they publish.
- Readers should be able to read and engage with whatever they want.
- Content should connect to the broader open-science publishing ecosystem, and respect and preserve the scientific record.

These principles do have room for interpretation and can be in tension with one another at times.
We'll try to document the rationale behind a decision we have made and link to relevant parts of our deployment so that others can learn how it was made and implemented.
